How to Become a Node Operator

Connect your GPU compute to the decentralized mesh in four straightforward steps and start receiving automated USDT micro-settlements.

01

Hardware Verification & VRAM Check

Ensure you have a supported NVIDIA GPU (RTX 3090, 4090, A100, or H100) running Ubuntu 22.04+ or modern Linux with proprietary NVIDIA drivers (v535+) and Docker Container Runtime installed. Minimum 24 GB VRAM is recommended to serve quantized open reasoning models.

02

Configure Self-Custodial Payout Wallet

Prepare any EVM-compatible wallet address on Ethereum, Arbitrum, or Base. Nexilo smart contracts disburse 90% of token fees atomically upon stream termination straight to this address with zero intermediary escrow.

03

One-Command Daemon Installation

Execute the verified operator daemon in terminal or detached container. The installer benchmarks your hardware, mounts flash attention kernels, and registers your node on the global latency mesh:

bash • install-node.sh 
curl -sSL https://nexilo.network/install-node.sh | bash -s -- --wallet 0xYourWalletAddress --gpu auto
04

Monitor Streaming Telemetry & Collect Yield

Open your local operator telemetry console at http://localhost:9100 to observe real-time inference sessions, thermal status, token delivery rates, and accumulated USDT settlement receipts.
