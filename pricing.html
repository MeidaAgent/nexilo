Frequently Asked Questions

What is the $2 hard ceiling per request?

Before an inference request streams, your client signs an authorization allowance capped at a maximum of $2.00 USDT. Under no circumstances can a request exceed this amount, preventing runaway loops or unexpected charges.

What happens if a node drops out during generation?

Because x402 tracks token streaming chunk-by-chunk with cryptographic hashes, you only settle the tokens you actually received. The orchestrator re-routes the remaining prompt to another node automatically.

Do node operators ever see or log my prompt?

Prompts are streamed in ephemeral memory buffers directly to the GPU execution context. Nexilo nodes run in sandboxed enclave containers with strict logging prohibitions enforced by consensus slashing.

How does payout settlement occur?

Settlement proofs are batched and posted atomically on any chain. 90% of the token fee is sent directly to the node operator's self-custodial wallet with zero middlemen withholding funds.

Zero Minimums. Ever.

Whether you run a single prototype request or stream millions of tokens in production, you pay the exact same transparent rate. We don't believe in forcing developers into expensive monthly tiers or enterprise contracts just to get fair pricing.

Enterprise Scale Quotas

Scaling up? Our decentralized mesh can handle thousands of concurrent requests. You can adjust your x402 spending ceiling on the fly. If you need dedicated node clusters or specialized SLA routing, contact our community validators for customized network routing profiles.
