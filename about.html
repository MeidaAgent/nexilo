Why We Built Nexilo

In 2024, it became clear that the future of artificial intelligence was being centralized into the hands of a few tech giants. Open-source models were improving rapidly, but the infrastructure to run them remained locked behind expensive, proprietary cloud APIs.

We started Nexilo to break that oligopoly. We envisioned a world where idle GPUs in homes and datacenters around the globe could be pooled together into an unstoppable, permissionless inference engine. By leveraging the x402 protocol, we aligned the incentives: developers get cheaper, reliable AI, and hardware owners get fair compensation.
